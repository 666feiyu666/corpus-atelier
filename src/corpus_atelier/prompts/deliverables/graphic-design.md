the named deliverable as an open design problem rather than forcing it into a poster or
cover template. Resolve hierarchy, copy, composition, typography, visual treatment,
viewing distance, crop safety, and reproduction constraints for that context.

exactly. Otherwise choose and justify an aspect ratio as a designer. The plan must name
the format, orientation, reduced aspect ratio, viewing context, safe area, and concise
