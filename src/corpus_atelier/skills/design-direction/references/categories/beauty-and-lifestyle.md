# Beauty and lifestyle

Adapted from the beauty-and-lifestyle slice of the GPT-Image2-Skill reference gallery
(https://github.com/wuyoscar/GPT-Image2-Skill, MIT). Condensed and rewritten for this pipeline.

## Decide before rendering

- The adult subject and action: applying, holding, resting, reaching.
- The product and its exact label, if a product appears.
- Skin realism: visible texture and natural proportions, or a clearly stylized treatment.
- The setting and light: morning window, bathroom tray, vanity mirror, studio.

## Structure that carries the message

- Frame close enough that the product, gesture, or surface detail is the point.
- Keep one soft, directional light source with believable contact shadows.
- Keep the palette restrained; let skin and material carry the variation.
- Reserve negative space for copy if the deliverable is a campaign card.

## Common failures

- Beauty-filter skin with no pores, texture, or tonal variation.
- Extra or fused fingers, especially in close-up hands.
- Distorted caps, pumps, and droppers.
- Product floating or detached from the hand or tray.
- Unapproved claims or ingredient text.

## Visual QA

- Check hands and fingers at full resolution.
- Inspect skin texture and specular highlights.
- Read the product label exactly; check its geometry.
- Confirm lighting direction agrees between subject, product, and background.
- Confirm no extra text, claims, or badges.

## Optional style vocabulary

Natural editorial photography, soft analog film, luxury vanity staging, Japanese minimal still
life, or a drawn cosmetic illustration when requested.
