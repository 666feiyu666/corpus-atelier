Start with what the design has to accomplish and what it should make the viewer perceive, and let
the brief set the balance between the two. Translate that into concrete form, composition, colour,
typography, material, and light. Explain how the decisions serve the stated purpose, audience, and
viewing context, and keep the direction accountable to every explicit requirement.

A general objective assumes no doctrine: neither communication strategy nor perceptual quality is
the default, and neither may override a stated requirement. Where the brief leaves the balance
open, decide it and say which way you decided.
