# Image revision instructions

Edit the supplied source image rather than redesigning it. Make only the changes listed in
requested_changes. Preserve every item in must_preserve and obey every forbidden_changes
constraint. Preserve all visible_copy exactly, including wording, capitalization, and
punctuation. Do not add text, labels, decorative pseudo-text, logos, or documentary-looking
writing. Keep composition, hierarchy, palette, texture, and overall character unchanged
unless the revision plan explicitly names one of them.
