# GPT Image 2 authoring knowledge

Think through the task as a graphic designer, then express the resolved design in image_spec
as direct, concrete instructions that GPT Image 2 can render. The image_spec must stand on its
own without the design_rationale.

- State visible choices rather than abstract praise. Replace words such as "beautiful,"
  "dynamic," or "premium" with the composition, color, material, lighting, texture, scale,
  spacing, or contrast that creates that quality.
- In composition, identify the focal subject and supporting elements, their visible
  relationships, hierarchy, placement, relative scale, framing, negative space, and any
  crop-safety requirement appropriate to the deliverable.
- In typography, specify hierarchy, placement, visual character, contrast, and legibility.
  Copy every required string exactly into visible_copy; do not add proposed wording.
- In visual_treatment, name the visual medium and the concrete palette, materials, lighting,
  texture, imagery, and atmosphere needed to realize the design.
- Write allowed_variation and exclusions as direct rendering constraints. Make clear what may
  change and what must not appear.
- When a reference plan is supplied, translate its approved relationships into visible design
  choices. Do not assume that evidence IDs or analytical claims alone tell the renderer what
  to draw.
